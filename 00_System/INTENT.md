# Intent

## Invariants

- **Provenance first**: external/source material is captured in `03_Sources/` with capture metadata; distilled docs cite the source.
- **Main functions are explicit**: hardware/runtime, DeX/Nebula conflict handling, dual-display isolation, BCI integration, and multimodal DeepStream fusion remain traceable to source and implementation notes.
- **Safety and device constraints are visible**: power/thermal limits, USB-C/DeX behavior, Android permission/runtime constraints, and optical HUD rules are documented before prototype claims.
- **No secrets**: never store account credentials, tokens, private keys, or personal health data exports; reference paths/systems only.
- **Owned by vrspark**: code, docs, ADRs, and experiments for this XR stack live here; Home keeps only registry/pointer-level cross-repo records.
