Build and document a practical, real-time multimodal XR stack around Nreal Light and Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra, with clear integration paths for OpenCode Mobile, a custom multi-pane HUD/UI, Neurosity Crown EEG, Galaxy Watch Ultra biometrics, and NVIDIA DeepStream fusion.
